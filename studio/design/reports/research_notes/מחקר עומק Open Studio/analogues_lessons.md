# Second-tier competitors and analogues for OPEN STUDIO: lessons on features, retention, monetisation and stalls (research notes, 2026-10-28)

Scope: players not covered in `competitors_global.md` and `business_models_cases.md`. Patreon, Bandcamp, Spotify for Artists and Behance were covered there only for **take rates**; this file adds product and growth lessons only. Method: web search only. Direct page fetches were blocked by the session's network allow-list for kunstaspekte.art, slash-paris.com, arteinformado.com, semafor.com, huxiu.com and are.na, so the facts below come from search-result summaries of the cited URLs. They should be spot-checked before they are quoted in a PDF.

## 1. Art-specific consumer apps and media: DailyArt, Art Fund / National Art Pass, Artips, Google Arts & Culture, Xiaohongshu "看展"

### Takeaway
In art, the consumer products that last are **habit-sized** (one artwork a day, one anecdote by email) or **membership-sized** (a pass that pays for itself in visits). Neither depends on a social graph. Viral moments (Art Selfie) bring downloads but do not build habit. In China the discovery layer for exhibitions is a general social app (Xiaohongshu), where people plan visits from photo-led guides.

### Cited Findings
**DailyArt (Moiseum / Zuzanna Stańska, Poland)**
- 100,000 downloads by July 2014, two years after the iOS launch — [British Council Voices, 2014](https://www.britishcouncil.org/voices-magazine/how-invent-mobile-app-people-love). An undated Android showcase story, probably from the mid-2010s, cites 135,000 downloads and 80,000+ members, and says Moiseum planned a subscription model and in-app purchases — [Android stories](https://android.com/intl/it_it/everyone/stories/dailyart).
- AppBrain, a third-party estimate seen 2026, puts DailyArt at **5M+ Google Play installs** — [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/dev/Moiseum/). A Polish article says the app reaches **800,000+ people a month**, with a 4.8/5 App Store rating — [National Geographic PL](https://www.national-geographic.pl/traveler/porady/polska-aplikacja-uznana-za-najlepsza-na-swiecie-mowi-sie-ze-jest-przenosnym-muzeum/).
- The app is free with in-app purchases, plus a paid club called **"DailyArt Patron"** — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dailyart/id547982045). DailyArt Magazine runs alongside as an editorial arm — [Muck Rack](https://muckrack.com/zuzanna-stanska).
- It won a **Cultural Impact** award in Apple's 2024 App Store Awards (announced 11 Dec 2024), "after 12 years of work" — [9to5Mac, 2024-12-11](https://9to5mac.com/2024/12/11/app-store-awards-winners-2024/); [TVP World](https://tvpworld.com/83981085/polish-art-app-wins-international-prize).

**Art Fund / National Art Pass (UK charity membership)**
- The National Art Pass launched in **April 2011**. Membership reached just under 90,000 by the end of 2011, up 15% — [Cassone, Jan 2012](https://www.cassone-art.com/art-news/2012/01/record-numbers-join-the-art-fund-to-boost-support-for-struggling-museums/). It passed 100,000 in 2012/13 — [Civil Society](https://www.civilsociety.co.uk/news/art-fund-on-track-to-boost-programme-with-18k-new-members.html) — and stood at **135,000 members in 2022/23** — [Art Fund](https://www.artfund.org/professional/news-and-insights/over-900-objects-and-works-of-art-to-enter-public-collections-with-art-fund-support).
- Prices: £83 a year in 2025 (£62.25 by direct debit), £50 for under-30s, £124 for a double pass; £87 in 2026. A 3-month trial for £20 was offered to first-time members in July–August 2025 — [Art Fund rates](https://artfund.org/national-art-pass/rates); [The Handbook](https://www.thehandbook.com/offers/explore-more-this-summer-with-a-3-month-national-art-pass/).
- What members get: free entry to hundreds of museums and **50% off major exhibitions**, and the fee funds acquisitions ("900+ objects"), so the purchase is part utility and part patronage — [Art Fund](https://www.artfund.org/professional/news-and-insights/over-900-objects-and-works-of-art-to-enter-public-collections-with-art-fund-support).

**Artips (France, newsletter)**
- Founded 2013 by Coline Debayle and Jean Perret. The founders first planned a mobile app and **chose a newsletter to ship faster**. They had 70,000 readers in 2014 — [Maddyness, 2014-11-13](https://www.maddyness.com/2014/11/13/artips-startup-art/) — then 250,000 subscribers — [Presse-Citron](https://www.presse-citron.net/artips-offrir-une-minute-dhistoire-de-lart-au-quotidien/) — and 500,000+ across Artips and Musiktips, per a 2017 media kit — [Sciencetips media kit, 2017](https://franceuniversites.fr/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Mediakit-Sciencetips_CPU.pdf).
- Revenue came from sponsored anecdotes, white-label versions for companies (BNP Paribas sent anecdotes to its staff every week) and a freemium app. Every text was checked by an art historian — [Maddyness](https://www.maddyness.com/2014/11/13/artips-startup-art/); [Time Out Paris](https://www.timeout.fr/paris/le-blog/avec-artips-lart-devoile-ses-petits-secrets-040416).

**Google Arts & Culture — Art Selfie**
- In mid-January 2018 the face-to-portrait matching feature took the app to **#1 free iPhone app in the US** (App Annie), and into the Android top 10. The feature was US-only at the time — [NPR, 2018-01-15](https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2018/01/15/578151195/google-app-goes-viral-making-an-art-out-of-matching-faces-to-paintings); [Euronews, 2018-01-15](https://www.euronews.com/2018/01/15/are-you-the-mona-lisa-google-app-lets-users-match-their-faces-to-famous-paintings). Commentators immediately urged people "not to delete" the app afterwards, which implies a large share installed it for the selfie alone — [Phaidon/Artspace](https://www.phaidon.com/en-ca/blogs/artspace/5-reasons-why-you-shouldnt-delete-google-arts-culture-after-finding-your-painting-doppelganger).

**Xiaohongshu (小红书) and "看展" (going to see exhibitions)**
- Xiaohongshu had 300M+ monthly active users by the end of 2023 — [Digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1260265.html) — and a non-official repost puts it at 320M in mid-2024 — [Xueqiu](https://xueqiu.com/8315851674/320853541/351728602).
- How exhibitions spread there: **photo-led "打卡" (check-in) posts in nine-photo grids, "攻略" (visit guides) and copied photo poses**. Art-school graduation shows went viral on Xiaohongshu and Weibo around 2023 as grid check-ins. By 2025 the spread had shifted to **single works becoming the hook**. Critics note that "must-check-in" lists bury works that don't photograph well — [Huxiu, 2026-08](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4885650.html); [Sohu, 2019](https://www.sohu.com/a/305087044_788514). Museums grow through meme-ified artefacts (for example, emoji packs of Han and Tang figurines) — [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/2888499371465351).
- The "plan first, then visit" behaviour is measurable on a neighbouring platform. Dianping's 2024 exhibitions and museums report shows notes and guides **up nearly 300% year on year**, with one posted every 20 seconds — [36Kr, Dianping 2024 report](https://www.36kr.com/p/3134927593052677).
- The downside: crowding and fights over photo spots at viral shows — [Wenhui Daily, 2023-02-22](https://dzb.whb.cn/imgPath/2023-02-22/90222.pdf).

### Inferences
- DailyArt grew from 100k to millions of installs over about a decade on a single daily ritual and editorial curation. That is a slow, durable curve. It took 12 years to reach Apple's editorial spotlight. OPEN STUDIO's "studio-journal clips" can play the same daily-ritual role if each day has one curated item.
- Art Fund shows that a **pass bundling discounts on paid shows** can be sold at ~£60–90 a year to a six-figure base in a market of ~67M people. In Israel the equivalent would be partnerships with museums (Tel Aviv Museum, Israel Museum) on discounts or priority access, sold as a "member" layer, rather than charging for app features.
- Artips is a precedent for **starting as a newsletter or WhatsApp channel before building an app**, and for B2B white-label revenue (corporate culture content).
- In Israel, as in China, discovery happens on an existing social app (Instagram, WhatsApp groups). OPEN STUDIO's exhibition pages should be built to be **shared as images**: a grid or single-work card with a link back.

### Gaps
- No official DailyArt figures for subscribers, Patron members or revenue. The 5M figure is a third-party estimate.
- No Art Fund membership figure after 2022/23, and no usage numbers for the Art Fund app.
- No current (2024–26) Artips subscriber count or company status.
- No hard Xiaohongshu data on the volume of 看展 notes. The Huxiu article could not be fetched directly (blocked).

## 2. Creator and marketplace platforms for visual artists: Saatchi Art, Artmajeur, Patreon, Behance, Instagram, Spotify for Artists, Bandcamp Daily, Are.na

### Takeaway
Supply-side tools succeed when they are **free, vain and useful** (portfolio, stats, a credited repost). Money comes from transactions (Saatchi, Artmajeur), from fans (Patreon) or from parent-company synergies (Behance via Creative Cloud). Are.na is the clearest small-scale proof that a no-ads, member-funded curation tool can survive on single-digit conversion.

### Cited Findings
- **Saatchi Art**: bought by Demand Media (later Leaf Group) in Aug 2014, then part of Graham Holdings after it acquired Leaf in 2021. It claims "100,000+ emerging artists from 100+ countries" (older boilerplate); another source says 80,000+ — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saatchi_Art); [adlibrary](https://adlibrary.com/brands/saatchi-art). No 2024–26 traffic data was found.
- **Artmajeur (France)**: YourArt took a majority stake in **Dec 2024**; the combined platform had 3.6M artworks and 130,000 artists — [BusinessWire, 2024-12-18](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241218124563/en/YourArt-Acquires-Artmajeur-Creating-a-New-Global-Player-in-the-Online-Art-Marketplace-ArtMajeur-by-YourArt). By **June 2025** it had 137,000 artists and 3.5M+ works, and the company positions itself as "boosted by AI" — [Silicon UK press release, 2025-06-04](https://www.silicon.co.uk/press-release/artmajeur-by-yourart-a-merger-boosted-by-ai). The commission rate was not found.
- **Patreon for visual artists**: about **284,000 creators at the end of 2024**, slightly down on 2023 (Graphtreon via Statista) — [Statista DE](https://de.statista.com/statistik/daten/studie/1357763/umfrage/anzahl-der-creator-auf-patreon-weltweit). An undated blog claims "250,000 visual artists"; that is unreliable and probably counts every account rather than active ones — [ArtWeb blog](https://blog.artweb.com/how-to/patreon-for-artists/). In 2022, 19% of visual artists said they built their first audience on Twitter, meaning Patreon monetises an audience built elsewhere — [VPNAlert citing Patreon 2022 census](https://vpnalert.com/resources/patreon-statistics/).
- **Behance (Adobe)**: **56M+ members (Dec 2024)**, up from ~24M in 2020 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behance). In Jan 2026 it added structured freelance job posts with AI matching and on-platform payment, moving from portfolio to hiring marketplace — [Behance blog, 2026-01](https://www.behance.net/blog/structured-job-posts). Adobe Portfolio sites come only with a Creative Cloud subscription, which ties the supply side to the parent's paid product — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behance).
- **Instagram** (Aug 2025): a credited **Reposts** tab that spreads work to non-followers; an opt-in **Map** for location-tagged posts and Stories nearby; a **Friends tab in Reels**. In April 2025 it tested "Lately", which auto-compiles a week of Stories into a Highlight — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-updates-reposts-location-friends/); [GSMArena](https://www.gsmarena.com/instagram_introduces_repost_and_location_sharing-news-68934.php); [Planoly, Apr 2025](https://planoly.com/blog/april-social-media-updates).
- **Spotify for Artists / Loud & Clear**: the annual Loud & Clear report (published 11 Mar 2026, covering 2025) reports that **13,800 artists earned $100k+** and 1,500+ earned $1M+ — [Billboard Canada, 2026-03](https://ca.billboard.com/business/streaming/spotify-says-over-13800-artists-generated-100000-on-the-platform-last-year-loud-clear-report). It is a transparency product aimed at the supply side, and it keeps artists defending the platform. The number of artists using Spotify for Artists was not found.
- **Bandcamp Daily**: editorial launched in **summer 2016** about artists on the platform — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandcamp). After Songtradr bought Bandcamp from Epic in Oct 2023 it laid off about half the staff, including part of the Daily's editorial team (reports range from 3 of 6 to "half"). Songtradr still publicly committed to keep "Bandcamp Fridays and Bandcamp Daily", a sign that editorial is seen as core to the brand — [Rolling Stone AU](https://au.rollingstone.com/?p=51320); [Billboard](https://ca.billboard.com/bandcamp-songtradr-sale); [Louder](https://loudersound.com/news/half-bandcamp-staff-laid-off).
- **Are.na**: in 2018 it raised **$270,206 from 849 investors** through equity crowdfunding on Republic (SAFE), with 40,000 members and **6%+ of active users paying for Premium** at the time — [Republic](https://republic.com/arena); [Are.na blog](https://are.na/blog/announcing-crowdfunding-campaign). Its own pages give **15,777** (About) and **20,516** (Roadmap) Premium supporters; the pages are from different dates and the figures conflict — [Are.na about](https://www.are.na/about); [Are.na roadmap](https://are.na/roadmap). It reports no other outside funding and plans to become a public benefit corporation — same sources.

### Inferences
- Instagram's Aug 2025 Map and Reposts cover part of OPEN STUDIO's "map + follow" idea for free. OPEN STUDIO's defensible difference is **structured, current exhibition data** (dates, venue, works list, 3D tour). Instagram's map shows posts, not shows.
- The Spotify Loud & Clear and Wrapped-for-artists pattern transfers directly: an annual "Your year on OPEN STUDIO" for artists and galleries (views, follows, saves, tour minutes), free, and built to be shared.
- Are.na shows that ~6% of active users will pay for a calm, ad-free tool. That fits the 3–8% freemium benchmark in `business_models_cases.md`.

### Gaps
- No Saatchi Art traffic or revenue for 2024–26; no Artmajeur commission rate; no Patreon visual-art share for 2025; no Spotify for Artists user count; current Are.na MAU not found.

## 4. Photo-social apps that surged and stalled: Vero and Glass

### Takeaway
"Ad-free, chronological, for real creatives" can cause a **spike** (Vero, Feb–Mar 2018), but a spike without a graph that stays, and without infrastructure ready for load, fades within weeks. A paid-from-day-one model (Glass) survives without VC but stays niche.

### Cited Findings
- **Vero**: launched in 2015. It had fewer than 150,000–200,000 users until Feb 2018, then went to **~3M registered users within days** (early March 2018) and **#1 on the US App Store** — [VOA News, 2018-03](https://www.voanews.com/a/vero-instagram-alternative/4278121.html); [Entrepreneur, 2018-03](https://www.entrepreneur.com/article/309748); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vero_(app)). Drivers: anger at Instagram's algorithmic feed and ads, plus the Cambridge Analytica news, and word of mouth in cosplay and tattoo communities — same sources. Servers crashed for days, and Vero extended its "free for life" offer in response — [Shropshire Star, 2018-03-01](https://shropshirestar.com/news/science-and-technology/2018/03/01/social-media-app-vero-extends-free-for-life-offer-after-days-of-technical-difficulties). Analysts described a "sudden rise and nearly as sudden fall" within about a month — [Tubefilter, 2018-03-12](https://www.tubefilter.com/2018/03/12/vero-rise-and-fall-lessons-better-app/); [Mention-Me, Apr 2018](https://www.mention-me.com/blog/the-rise-of-social-media-app-zero). No later active-user figures were found.
- **Glass**: founded by Tom Watson and Stefan Borsje. It launched Aug 2021 with an invite-only waitlist and opened to all iOS users soon after. It costs **$4.99/month or $29.99/year**, has no ads, no VC and no public like counts — [TechCrunch, 2021-09-03](https://techcrunch.com/2021/09/03/instagram-may-not-be-a-photo-sharing-app-anymore-but-glass-is); [PetaPixel, 2021-08-12](https://petapixel.com/2021/08/12/glass-is-a-subscription-based-photo-sharing-app-for-photographers). A 2021 review praised the mission but criticised the design — [PetaPixel, 2021-08-27](https://petapixel.com/2021/08/27/glass-photo-app-review-design-needs-work-but-the-mission-is-clear). No subscriber numbers were ever published that I could find.

### Inferences
- The lesson for OPEN STUDIO: being "anti-Instagram" is not a reason to come back daily. Each return needs a **utility** (what's on this week near me, what's new from artists I follow) and a **personal record** (what I saw).
- Glass's price (~$30/year) and Art Fund's (~£60–90/year) bracket what an engaged art audience will pay for a calm, ad-free experience. In both cases the ask is backed by a clear "why" (support photographers, fund museums).

### Gaps
- No post-2018 Vero MAU; no Glass subscriber or revenue figures.

## 5. Logging analogues: Letterboxd, Strava, Untappd, Goodreads/StoryGraph — applicable to "log the exhibitions you visited"

### Takeaway
Letterboxd is the strongest analogue. A **diary of what you watched**, ratings, **lists** and an annual **Year in Review** turned a solitary activity into a social identity. It grew from 1.8M (2020) to 30M+ members (2026) on subscriptions, ads and partnerships. Strava and Untappd show the same loop with kudos, clubs and badges. The risk: putting the annual recap behind a paywall (Strava, 2025) caused a backlash.

### Cited Findings
**Letterboxd (NZ)**
- Founded by Matthew Buchanan and Karl von Randow and launched in 2011. The two founding words were **"Diary" and "Lists"** — [The Ringer, 2020-09-18](https://www.theringer.com/2020/09/18/movies/letterboxd-film-discussion-site-streaming-movies). It turned down early VC and became **profitable only in 2019** — [University of Auckland, 2023-11-07](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/news/2023/11/07/letterboxd-founders-smash-hit.html).
- Growth: **1.8M users in 2020 → ~10M by late 2023**, "turbo-charged" by the pandemic — [U. Auckland, 2023-11](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/news/2023/11/07/letterboxd-founders-smash-hit.html). **21.4M (Q2 2025, +47% YoY)** — [StockTitan, Tiny Q2 2025](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/TNYZF/tiny-reports-q2-2025-1j7jkc79msol.html). **29M+ (Q1 2026)** — [Tiny](https://tiny.com/companies/letterboxd). **30M+ (July 2026)** — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letterboxd).
- Tiny bought 60% in 2023 at a valuation of ~$50–60M — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/letterboxd-sells-a-majority-stake-after-explosive-pandemic-fueled-growth-201646444.html); [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/letterboxds-canadian-owner-reportedly-looking-to-sell/). In 2026 it was reportedly exploring a sale via LionTree at ~$250M, with reported interest from Netflix, Sony and Paramount — [Semafor, 2026-04-26](https://semafor.com/article/04/26/2026/whats-next-for-letterboxd); [Tribune PK](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2617555/letterboxd-reportedly-draws-takeover-interest-from-netflix-sony-and-paramount?amp=1) (unconfirmed reports).
- Revenue: subscriptions (Pro ~$18.99/yr, Patron ~$48.99/yr, per a third-party paywall library), advertising and partnerships. Tiny named Letterboxd a primary driver of its fund revenue growth in Dec 2025 — [Adapty paywall library](https://adapty.io/paywall-library/letterboxd/); [Kavout summary of Tiny filings](https://www.kavout.com/market-lens/tiny-s-paradox-divesting-letterboxd-to-unlock-hidden-value). **Pro/Patron members see their stats all year; everyone gets the annual Year in Review** (released 2 Jan 2026 for 2025) — [Rotek.fr](https://rotek.fr/letterboxd-wrapped-2025-comment-acceder-year-in-review/).

**Strava**
- About $500M ARR reported around its May 2025 Series F; it was reported to be preparing an IPO — [TechCrunch, 2025-10-12](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/12/strava-eyes-ipo-as-gen-z-trades-dating-apps-for-running-clubs/); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/strava). Kudos turn workouts into "social currency" — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/12/strava-eyes-ipo-as-gen-z-trades-dating-apps-for-running-clubs/).
- **Clubs** almost quadrupled in 2025 to 1M in total, and club events rose 1.5×. 55% of Gen Z athletes name social connection as the main reason they join groups (company data) — [Bikeradar, Year in Sport 2025](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/strava-year-in-sport-2025); [Webull/Strava press release](https://www.webull.com/news/13961203642123264).
- **Year in Sport moved behind the subscription (~$80/yr) in Dec 2024 and drew user backlash**. Strava kept uploads, kudos and community free — [road.cc](https://road.cc/content/news/strava-year-sport-now-only-subscribers-317425).

**Untappd (beer check-ins)**
- 1M users in Jan 2014 and 3.2M by Apr 2016. Its **billionth check-in came in 2021**. It uses **badges** (beer, venue, location, special), and venue check-ins at breweries and festivals earn badges — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Untappd); [Brewbound](https://www.brewbound.com/news/nextglass-ceo-shares-optimism-for-craft-recovery-during-ba-collab-hour). Money comes from the supply side: ~20,000 "Untappd for Business" venue partners (third-party listing) — [SoftwareOne](https://platform.softwareone.com/product/untappd/PCP-5148-2389).

**Goodreads / StoryGraph**
- Goodreads only refreshed its logo and icon in July 2025 after years of criticism that it looked dated; StoryGraph courts its users with a one-tap Goodreads import — [weak aggregator source](https://www.lojasouparceiro.ticketlog.com.br/celebrity-radar-news-hub-042/7-essential-goodreads-updates-the-storygraph-challenge-you-need-to-know-for-2025-tx4az.html); [StoryGraph App Store](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/storygraph-reading-tracker/1570489264?country=it). Treat this as weak evidence: logging apps that stop investing open the door to challengers, and import is the wedge.

### Inferences
- An "exhibition log" would be much lower-frequency than film: people visit perhaps 5–30 shows a year, against 100+ films for heavy Letterboxd users. So the log must also capture **works seen, artists followed and tours watched**, not just visits, so the Year in Review has enough material.
- Letterboxd took 9 years to reach 1.8M users and grew mostly when an external shock (the pandemic) pushed the core activity. Logging products compound slowly; OPEN STUDIO should not expect to break out quickly from logging alone.
- Untappd's model (users log for free, **venues pay** for a verified page, menu and analytics) maps directly onto galleries paying for a verified venue page plus visitor analytics.

### Gaps
- No official Letterboxd subscriber count or revenue; no current Untappd MAU; no data on Goodreads Reading Challenge participation; no data on how Strava segments affect retention.

## 6. Event discovery and invitations: Fever, DICE, Partiful

### Takeaway
The event winners own **one irreplaceable step**. Fever owns curated discovery plus its own content (Candlelight-style formats); DICE owns the phone-bound ticket with no fees; Partiful owns the frictionless invite link. Each turns every attendee into a distribution channel.

### Cited Findings
- **Fever (Spain/US)**: raised $227M at a $1B valuation (Dec 2021) — [PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/Fever-raises-227M); $110M at ~$1.8–2B (Jan 2023) — [IQ Magazine](https://www.iqmagazine.com/?p=302505); $100M (Jun 2025). A **$250M** raise in 2026 implied a valuation of ~€4.5B ($5.2B) through Atresmedia's stake sale. It claims 300M+ people "discovered experiences" in the past year and says it is EBITDA-positive (company claims) — [Australasian Leisure Management, 2026](https://www.ausleisure.com.au/news/fever-raises-us250-million-to-accelerate-global-expansion).
- **DICE (UK)**: bought by Fever in **June 2025**. In the prior two years it had doubled ticket sales and passed **10M monthly active fans**. Its model: tickets locked to the phone (anti-tout), no booking fees, waitlists and fan-to-fan returns. 2022 revenue was $28.5M (+359%) against a $51.1M operating loss — [Pollstar, 2025-06-05](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/06/05/ticketer-dice-acquired-by-events-discovery-platform-fever/); [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/fever-buys-ticketing-app-dice/).
- **Partiful (US)**: guests RSVP from a **link with no app and no account**, and get **text reminders**. The link is shared in iMessage, Instagram or WhatsApp, and every invite advertises Partiful to the guests. Core features are free, with paid add-ons later — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/partiful); [Kristen Berman](https://kristenberman.substack.com/p/3-ways-partiful-is-beating-eventbrite). It reported 2M new users in 2025; Sensor Tower estimated ~500k MAU in Q1 2025 (+400% YoY), and growth continued after **Apple Invites** launched in Feb 2025 — [CNBC via NBC, 2025](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/business/money-report/meet-partiful-the-gen-z-party-planning-staple-thats-taking-on-apple/6231135/).

### Inferences
- An exhibition opening (vernissage) is OPEN STUDIO's "party". A Partiful-style **opening invite link** (RSVP without an app, reminder on the day, see who's going), issued by the gallery or artist, could be the main acquisition loop. It is more promising than SEO or app-store discovery.
- DICE's losses before its exit warn that ticketing is capital-intensive. For OPEN STUDIO, RSVP and reminders are enough; payment can come later.

### Gaps
- No Fever revenue figures; Partiful funding not verified here; exact date of the 2026 Fever round not confirmed.

## 8. Transferable product mechanisms for OPEN STUDIO (with evidence)

### Takeaway
Fourteen mechanisms have evidence behind them. The strongest for an early, Israel-first art app are: a **personal log** (Letterboxd), a **free annual recap built to be shared** (Wrapped / Year in Review), **a link-based invite with no app needed for openings** (Partiful), **a daily single-item ritual** (DailyArt) and **a venue pays, visitor logs** split (Untappd).

### Cited Findings (mechanism → evidence → OPEN STUDIO version)
1. **Diary/log of what you saw** (Letterboxd "Diary") → founding concept; 1.8M → 30M+ members in 2020–2026 — [The Ringer](https://www.theringer.com/2020/09/18/movies/letterboxd-film-discussion-site-streaming-movies); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letterboxd). → "I was here": log a show, with a date, a rating or a favourite work.
2. **User lists** (Letterboxd "Lists"; Are.na channels) → Letterboxd's second founding word; Are.na built a paying base (6%+ of active users in 2018) on channels — [Republic](https://republic.com/arena). → "My Tel Aviv gallery route", "Best painting shows of 2026".
3. **Annual recap built to be shared** (Spotify Wrapped) → 200M engaged users and 500M shares in its first 24 hours (Dec 2025); in 2024 it took ~62 hours to reach 200M — [TechCrunch, 2025-12-04](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/04/spotify-says-wrapped-2025-is-its-biggest-yet-with-200m-users-in-its-first-day); [Music Week](https://www.musicweek.com/digital/read/spotify-wrapped-2025-was-biggest-ever-with-200-million-engaged-users/093178). → "Your art year": shows, artists, cities. Keep it **free**; Strava's paywalled recap drew backlash — [road.cc](https://road.cc/content/news/strava-year-sport-now-only-subscribers-317425).
4. **Supply-side recap and transparency** (Spotify for Artists / Loud & Clear) → an annual public report on artist earnings — [Billboard CA, 2026-03](https://ca.billboard.com/business/streaming/spotify-says-over-13800-artists-generated-100000-on-the-platform-last-year-loud-clear-report). → "Your year on OPEN STUDIO" for artists and galleries, plus an annual Israeli art-scene report as PR.
5. **Link invite with no app** (Partiful) → ~500k MAU in Q1 2025 (+400%), 2M new users in 2025 — [CNBC/NBC](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/business/money-report/meet-partiful-the-gen-z-party-planning-staple-thats-taking-on-apple/6231135/). → Opening-night RSVP links that galleries share on WhatsApp and Instagram.
6. **Lightweight social approval** (Strava kudos) → described as core "social currency" — [TechCrunch, 2025-10-12](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/12/strava-eyes-ipo-as-gen-z-trades-dating-apps-for-running-clubs/). → One-tap "applause" on an artist's studio clip, visible to the artist only, which avoids public vanity counts (the Glass approach).
7. **Clubs and group events** (Strava clubs) → 1M clubs, ~4× new clubs in 2025; club events 1.5× — [Bikeradar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/strava-year-in-sport-2025). → Gallery-walk groups and art clubs with a shared calendar.
8. **Badges for places visited** (Untappd venue badges) → billionth check-in in 2021 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Untappd). → "Visited 10 Jaffa galleries" or "Saw every show at Gallery Week". Use sparingly.
9. **Venue pays, visitor is free** (Untappd for Business, ~20k venues) — [SoftwareOne](https://platform.softwareone.com/product/untappd/PCP-5148-2389). → Galleries pay for a verified page, analytics and opening tools.
10. **Daily single-item ritual** (DailyArt; Artips) → 5M+ installs (estimate) and an Apple Cultural Impact award in 2024; Artips reached 250k–500k subscribers — [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/dev/Moiseum/); [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2024/12/11/app-store-awards-winners-2024/); [Sciencetips kit](https://franceuniversites.fr/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Mediakit-Sciencetips_CPU.pdf). → "Today in the studio": one clip a day, also sent as a WhatsApp or email digest.
11. **Membership that pays for itself** (Art Fund pass: 50% off exhibitions; £83/yr; 135k members) — [Art Fund](https://artfund.org/national-art-pass/rates). → Paid "Friend" tier with partner-museum discounts and priority opening RSVPs.
12. **Credited reposts, posts that travel** (Instagram Reposts, Aug 2025; Xiaohongshu nine-grid check-ins) — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-updates-reposts-location-friends/); [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4885650.html). → Auto-generated share cards (a grid of works or a single work, with credit and link) for Instagram Stories.
13. **Playful viral hook with a shareable result** (Google Art Selfie, #1 free US app, Jan 2018) — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2018/01/15/578151195/google-app-goes-viral-making-an-art-out-of-matching-faces-to-paintings). → Use only as a launch spike, with a follow-up hook, since retention after the selfie was doubtful.
14. **Editorial arm as brand moat** (Bandcamp Daily, kept even through 50% layoffs in 2023) — [Billboard](https://ca.billboard.com/bandcamp-songtradr-sale). → A short weekly "what to see" editorial, which is also SEO and newsletter content.
15. **Community ownership and funding** (Are.na: $270k from 849 users on Republic, 2018) — [Republic](https://republic.com/arena). → An option for a mission-driven Israeli art community round once there is a core of users.

### Inferences
- Ranked for the first 12 months: (5) opening links and (12) share cards for acquisition; (1) log and (10) daily ritual for retention; (9) venue-pays for revenue; (3) annual recap as the year-end PR moment, once there is a year of logs.
- Things to avoid, from the evidence: a paywalled recap (Strava backlash), relying on an "anti-Instagram" spike (Vero), and one-off novelty features without a loop (Art Selfie).

### Gaps
- No causal evidence (A/B tests) found for any mechanism's effect on retention; the evidence is correlational or company-reported.
- Partiful's and Letterboxd's retention curves are not public.
