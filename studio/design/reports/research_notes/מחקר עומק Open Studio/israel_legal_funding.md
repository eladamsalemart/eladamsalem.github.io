# Israeli legal, regulatory and funding environment for launching OPEN STUDIO

Research date: 28.10.2026. 15 web searches, in Hebrew and English. Many hits were vendor or blog guides, not primary texts, and are marked as such. This is not legal advice. Each section says where a lawyer or accountant is required.

## 1. Copyright: reproducing artworks in exhibition photos and 3D tours

### Takeaway
Israeli law has no exception that clearly covers OPEN STUDIO's core use: systematic, commercial reproduction of other artists' works, temporarily on show in a gallery, inside photos and 3D tours. Section 23 (freedom of panorama) covers only works *permanently* located in a *public* place, and only architecture, sculpture and applied art. Section 22 (incidental use) does not fit works that are the subject of the shot. Section 19 (fair use) is uncertain for a commercial platform. Written licences from the artists (copyright plus moral-rights terms) and from the photographers are therefore needed before launch.

### Cited Findings
- **Section 19 (fair use).** The purposes listed are open-ended (research, criticism, reviews, journalistic reporting and others). The factors weighed are the purpose and character of the use, the nature of the work, the scope of the use, and the effect on the work's value and potential market. — [Copyright Act 2007, English translation (TAU, Birnhack)](https://www.tau.ac.il/law/members/birnhack/IsraeliCopyrightAct2007.pdf)
- **Section 22 (incidental use).** It permits incidental use of a work in a photograph, film or sound recording, and use of the work in which it was incidentally captured. Deliberate inclusion of musical works is excluded. — [Copyright Act 2007 (TAU)](https://www.tau.ac.il/law/members/birnhack/IsraeliCopyrightAct2007.pdf)
- **How 19 and 22 relate.** Birnhack and Elkin-Koren: a truly incidental use may fall under s.22. Otherwise the use must fit s.19 through a fair-use analysis. Israeli courts have read fair use broadly, for example a lower court held that posting copyrighted photos in a non-commercial school newsletter was fair. — [InfoJustice: Limitations and Exceptions to Copyright in Israel](https://infojustice.org/archives/20276); [InfoJustice 2012 PDF](https://infojustice.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/05/Israel-v-May-2012.pdf)
- **Section 23 (freedom of panorama).** Broadcasting, or copying by photograph, drawing, sketch or similar visual depiction, of an architectural work, a sculpture or a work of applied art is permitted if the work is located *permanently* in a *public place*. — [Official gazette, Copyright Law 2007 (Knesset)](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/17/law/17_lsr_300007.pdf)
- **Street-art case.** Tel Aviv Magistrates' Court, 9.12.2024, Civ. 39402-10-19, Kochava v. Dahan Peretz: making art in public space does not reduce its protection as a work, and not every use of street art is permitted. The plaintiffs also claimed moral-rights infringement for removing the credit. — [Appeldorf & Co.: Copyright in street art](https://anc-law.co.il/2024/12/%D7%96%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%99%D7%95%D7%A6%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A8%D7%97%D7%95%D7%91/)
- **Moral rights.** They belong by law only to the author of the work. A use that is permitted economically can still infringe moral rights, for example through a missing credit or a distortion. — [Appeldorf & Co.](https://anc-law.co.il/2024/12/%D7%96%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%99%D7%95%D7%A6%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A8%D7%97%D7%95%D7%91/)
- **Copyright in photographs and its uncertain scope in Israeli case law.** — [computer-law.co.il: Uncertainty in the scope of copyright protection for photographs](https://www.computer-law.co.il/?item=371&section=94); [GSIP: Copyright in photographic works](https://www.gsip.co.il/%D7%96%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%99%D7%95%D7%A6%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D/%D7%99%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9D/)
- **Recent photo-infringement case (Feb 2026).** Civ. 34457-02-24, Traklin Systems v. Yedioth Ahronoth. I did not read the outcome. — [law.co.il](https://www.law.co.il/computer-law/2026/02/27/traklin-systems-ltd-v-yedioth-ahronot-ltd/)
- **AI training.** The Ministry of Justice opinion (2022) holds that using copyrighted works for machine learning will generally be fair use under s.19, with exceptions. It is an interpretive opinion, not a ruling. — [Herzog: MoJ opinion on ML](https://herzoglaw.co.il/en/news-and-insights/ministry-of-justice-opinion-on-the-use-of-copyrighted-works-for-machine-learning-purposes/); [Pearl Cohen](https://www.pearlcohen.com/israeli-ministry-of-justice-opines-on-fair-use-of-copyrighted-content-for-ai-ml-training/)
- **Overview of the Israeli copyright regime.** — [Chambers Copyright Israel 2021 (law.co.il)](https://www.law.co.il/media/knowledge-centers/chambers-copyright-israel-2021.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Section 23 does not cover exhibitions.** A gallery show is temporary, a gallery is arguably not a "public place", and paintings, photographs and installations are not on the s.23 list. Only a permanent public sculpture or building would be covered.
- **Section 22 covers only the background.** It helps with works caught in the edge of a frame. It does not help when the works are the reason the tour exists. In a 3D tour every work in the room is displayed on purpose.
- **Section 19 is weak for this use.** It is a case-by-case defence. For a commercial platform that charges galleries and artists, the "market effect" and "commercial purpose" factors weigh against it.
- **Licences needed.** Practical recommendation (for a lawyer to draft):
  - a licence agreement for each exhibiting artist: non-exclusive, worldwide, covering digital display, 3D reconstruction and promotion, with an explicit clause on credit and integrity (moral rights) and a right to remove;
  - a licence or assignment from the photographer whose photos feed the tour. Under Israeli law the photographer is the default owner, unless they are an employee or there is an agreement;
  - an agreement with the gallery that confirms it holds or has obtained rights from its artists, and an indemnity;
  - an explicit no-AI-training / opt-out clause, to build trust with artists.
- **Moral-rights risk in 3D.** Reconstruction (for example the "realistic canvas sides" and baked light) could be argued to be a "distortion" or "modification" of the work. An approval step by the artist, plus a takedown mechanism, reduces this risk.
- **For EU/US expansion.** The EU DSM Directive (2019/790) art. 14 (reproductions of public-domain visual art), the panorama exceptions in InfoSoc art. 5(3)(h) (optional and different in each country), and the US fair-use doctrine all need a separate review. These were not searched in this round.

### Gaps
- I found no Israeli ruling specifically on photographing or 3D-scanning artworks inside a gallery exhibition. A search on Nevo or with a copyright lawyer is required.
- I did not check the outcome of the Kochava ruling or the Traklin ruling in full.
- I did not search the EU DSM Directive, panorama exceptions by country, the US fair-use case law (Warhol v. Goldsmith 2023), or AI opt-outs (DSM art. 4(3)) in this round. Not sourced.

## 2. Privacy: Amendment 13 to the Privacy Protection Law, faces, location, GDPR

### Takeaway
Since 14.8.2025, most private databases no longer need registration. A small app does not need a DPO unless it falls into one of the specified categories. It still must:
- give notice under s.11 (a privacy policy);
- comply with the Information Security Regulations 2017;
- sign written agreements with its processors (hosting, analytics);
- handle "information of special sensitivity" with care.

Fines are now real.

### Cited Findings
- **In force.** Amendment 13 came into force on 14.8.2025. — [alaw.co.il complete guide](https://alaw.co.il/privacy/law/amendment-13/); [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/law/article-1214933)
- **Registration has narrowed.** It remains mandatory mainly for a public body's database and for a data broker's database. A database exempt from registration is not exempt from the other duties. — [iCount guide](https://www.icount.co.il/blog/amendment-13/); [Chamber of Commerce](https://www.chamber.org.il/serviceslobby/legal/74023/164240/)
  - Meitar's 2024 update describes registration as remaining for direct-mail databases holding information on 10,000+ people and for public bodies. — [Meitar: Amendment 13 – major points (HEB)](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-to-the-Privacy-Protection-Law-Approved-by-the-Knesset-Major-Points-of-the-Amendment-and-Its-Implications-HEB.pdf)
  - **Discrepancy:** "data broker" vs "direct-mail ≥10,000". The final law is worded as data broker, so verify against the text.
- **Notification duty.** Anyone holding information of special sensitivity on more than 100,000 people must notify the Authority within 30 days, with the DPO's details and the database definitions document (s.8A(b)). — [iCount](https://www.icount.co.il/blog/amendment-13/)
- **DPO.** Required for public bodies, banks, direct-mail service providers and bodies that process significant volumes of information. This is set by the type of activity, not by business size. — [Meitar Data Privacy Day 2025 (HEB)](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/In-honor-of-International-Data-Privacy-Day-–-Key-updates-in-the-field-of-privacy-in-Israel-HEB.pdf); [sec-it](https://sec-it.co.il/%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%9F-13-%D7%9C%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%94%D7%92%D7%A0%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%98%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%9B%D7%9C-%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7-%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A9/)
- **Duties on every database controller, regardless of size.** The duty to inform (s.11), the information security duties, and a written agreement with processors. — [iCount](https://www.icount.co.il/blog/amendment-13/)
- **Fines.**
  - The Authority now has the power to impose financial sanctions, which vary by the provision breached, the number of data subjects, the size of the organisation and the level of security. — [Gornitzky (PDF)](https://www.gornitzky.co.il/wp-content/uploads/pdf/post/30434_----------.pdf)
  - Example: failing to register or notify, NIS 150,000 (a table in a secondary guide). — [iCount](https://www.icount.co.il/blog/amendment-13/)
  - A few months after the amendment took effect, no significant fines had yet been imposed (late-2025 report). — [Maariv, Adv. Yaakov Oz](https://www.maariv.co.il/economy/israel/article-1278877)
- **New definition.** The amendment added the term "information of special sensitivity". — [Meitar](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Amendment-13-to-the-Privacy-Protection-Law-Approved-by-the-Knesset-Major-Points-of-the-Amendment-and-Its-Implications-HEB.pdf)
- **Further practical guides (secondary).** — [hgj.co.il](https://hgj.co.il/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%94%D7%92%D7%A0%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%98%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%9F-13/); [mindfulwebnerd 2026](https://mindfulwebnerd.com/tikun-13/); [RSM Israel](https://www.rsm.global/israel/en/node/228)

### Inferences
- **What applies to OPEN STUDIO at launch.** Accounts, email, follows and analytics on a few thousand users:
  - most likely no registration and no DPO;
  - but it still needs a Hebrew/English privacy policy (s.11 notice: purpose, whether providing the data is required by law, who receives it);
  - a "database definitions document";
  - security measures under the 2017 regulations (most likely the "basic" security level for a small database: access control, backups, logging, breach handling);
  - DPAs with its cloud and analytics providers (GitHub, Cloudflare, Google, etc.).
- **Location.** Precise user location is better treated as sensitive information. Rely on explicit, real-time consent (browser/iOS permission), do not store a location history, and do not share it with third parties.
- **Faces in tour photos.** A face is "information" about a person, and potentially biometric information (special sensitivity) if processed for identification. A simple practical solution is automatic face blurring in the pipeline before publication. This is also the norm at Google Street View / Matterport.
- **Minors.** The terms of use should set a minimum age (16 or 18) for opening an account, to avoid needing parental consent.
- **GDPR.** It applies once the app targets or monitors EU users (art. 3(2)). It then requires a lawful basis, rights of access and erasure, and possibly an EU representative (art. 27). Israel has an EU adequacy decision, which makes transfers easier.
- **Apple.** Before an App Store submission, fill in the App Privacy "nutrition label", and from iOS 17 a privacy manifest for SDKs.
- **Not a source.** GDPR, the EU adequacy decision and the Apple labels come from general knowledge. They were not searched in this round.

### Gaps
- I did not find a dedicated guideline from the Privacy Protection Authority for small businesses, or on location or minors. Read gov.il/privacy directly.
- I have no sourced answer on whether faces in exhibition photos (without identification processing) are "biometric information" under Amendment 13.
- No current source on the exact Information Security Regulations security levels after Amendment 13.
- No source for GDPR, the EU adequacy decision or the Apple privacy labels in this round.
- The Privacy Law's rule on publishing a person's image (s.2(4), a photo published in circumstances that may humiliate) is not sourced here.

## 3. Accessibility: Israeli Standard 5568 / WCAG AA for websites and apps

### Takeaway
The accessibility regulations (Reg. 35) apply to internet services, explicitly including applications. They require compliance with Israeli Standard 5568 (WCAG 2.0 AA). A VAT-exempt dealer, or a business with average annual turnover up to NIS 100,000, is exempt from the website accessibility adjustments. Once OPEN STUDIO exceeds that threshold, or registers as a company, the app must be accessible and must publish an accessibility statement. Lawsuits and class actions are a real risk even for small businesses.

### Cited Findings
- **The exemption.** A VAT-exempt dealer, or one with average annual turnover not exceeding NIS 100,000, is exempt from the accessibility adjustments for internet services (Reg. 35F(g)). — [Computec: Reg. 35](https://www.computec.co.il/article/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%AA-%D7%A0%D7%92%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%90%D7%AA%D7%A8%D7%99-%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%A0%D7%98-%D7%A1%D7%A2-35-%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A0/); [ISOC-IL: All about the accessibility regulations](https://www.isoc.org.il/freedom-of-internet/accessibility/all-about-accessibility)
- **Conflict on the averaging period** (3 years vs. 3 months). — [ronen-web](https://ronen-web.com/website-accessibility-law-israel/); [vee.co.il 2026](https://vee.co.il/accessibility-law-2026/)
- **A further exemption for older services.** One source mentions an exemption for businesses up to NIS 1,000,000 for a website or application operating before the regulations took effect (Reg. 35F(i)). It is single-sourced and needs checking. It is not relevant to a new app.
- **Applications are covered.** The regulations refer to "application" as well as "website". — [ISOC-IL](https://www.isoc.org.il/freedom-of-internet/accessibility/all-about-accessibility)
- **Lawsuits.**
  - A class action may be filed where a site serves a broad public. — [DiGitale: penalties for an inaccessible site](https://digitale.co.il/he/%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%A9-%D7%90%D7%95-%D7%94%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%A1-%D7%A2%D7%9C-%D7%90%D7%AA%D7%A8-%D7%9C%D7%90-%D7%A0%D7%92%D7%99%D7%A9/)
  - Exempt businesses can also be sued. — [sgo.co.il 2026](https://sgo.co.il/en/blog/website-accessibility-israel-2026/)
- **Accessibility guides.** — [Israsky: Standard 5568 guide](https://www.israsky.co.il/negishut-atarim-teken-5568-madrich/)

### Inferences
- **What to do anyway.** Build to WCAG 2.1 AA now. It is cheap at an early stage and needed for the EU (European Accessibility Act, in force from 6.2025) and the US (ADA). It also takes away the main argument in Israeli lawsuits.
- **Hard parts in this app.**
  - The 3D tour: provide an alternative, meaning a list of works with alt text and descriptions, plus keyboard navigation.
  - Video clips: captions.
  - The map: a text list of exhibitions as an alternative.
- **Avoid overlay plugins.** "Accessibility plugins" (overlays) do not guarantee compliance. They are criticised internationally (my assessment, not sourced).
- **Accessibility statement.** Publish one with an accessibility coordinator's contact details.

### Gaps
- I did not check the text of the regulations on Nevo (the exact turnover calculation and the definition of "service provider" for an app).
- I found no reliable data on the amount of statutory damages without proof of damage (Equal Rights Law s.19N, up to NIS 50,000 is commonly cited) or on the number of class actions. Not sourced.

## 4. Business: consumer protection, VAT, invoicing, payments, business entity, trademark

### Takeaway
- **Business entity.** Start as a VAT-exempt dealer (ceiling NIS 122,833 in 2026), or a licensed dealer if selling to businesses (galleries) who expect a tax invoice. Move to a limited company once there are investors or grants. The Innovation Authority usually requires a company.
- **Invoicing.** From 1.6.2026 an allocation number is required on B2B invoices above NIS 5,000 before VAT.
- **Payments.** Stripe is still not available to Israeli businesses (as of 7/2026). Use a local gateway (Cardcom, Tranzila, Grow/Meshulam), or a Merchant of Record such as Paddle.
- **Subscriptions.** Disclose the right to cancel and how to do it, stop billing within 3–6 business days, and send an annual billing summary in March.
- **Trademark.** "OpenStudio" is a known mark (NREL energy software, ®), and "Open Studio(s)" is a generic term in the art world, which points to a weak mark. A lawyer's search is required.

### Cited Findings
- **Exempt-dealer ceiling 2026.** NIS 122,833 annual turnover. Above it, registration as a licensed dealer is mandatory. — [Green Invoice: exempt-dealer ceiling](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/magazine/תקרת-עוסק-פטור/); [Bizportal: licensed dealer](https://www.bizportal.co.il/guides/news/article/20039167)
- **Israel invoice model: allocation number.**
  - Stages: NIS 25,000 (5.2024), NIS 20,000 (2025), NIS 10,000 (1.1.2026), NIS 5,000 before VAT (from 1.6.2026).
  - Applies to B2B only, not to sales to private customers.
  - Sources: [Green Invoice: Israel invoice model 2026](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/magazine/israel-invoice/); [Horizon](https://horizon.org.il/cfo-outsource/allocation-number-invoice-2026/); [Baker Tilly](https://bakertilly.co.il/blog-invoices-2026.html); [basecrm](https://basecrm.co.il/guides/allocation-number-invoices-2026/)
  - **Note:** one guide in the results used 17% VAT in an example. VAT is 18% from 1.1.2025 (see Gaps).
- **Stripe.**
  - Not on its list of supported countries (check 7/2026).
  - Stripe is hiring a GM for Israel and building out a local team, with no launch date.
  - In the past it struggled to obtain an Israeli acquiring licence.
  - Workarounds: a US LLC, or a Merchant of Record such as Paddle, which supports Israel.
  - Sources: [adircpa: Stripe for an Israeli business](https://adircpa.com/en/guides/stripe-israel-tax); [Globes: Stripe to launch Israel operations](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-payments-giant-stripe-to-launch-israel-operations-1001560134); [Globes: clearing licence](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-stripe-struggling-to-receive-israeli-clearing-license-1001381078); [danielmashkov 2026 guide](https://danielmashkov.com/insights/website-payment-integration-israel); [route38](https://blog.route38.co.il/2024/09/03/accepting-payment-for-your-israeli-business/)
- **Cancelling an ongoing transaction (subscription).**
  - The consumer may cancel even after the service has started.
  - The business must stop charging within 3–6 business days, depending on how the notice was given.
  - The business must disclose the right to cancel and the channels for doing so (address, phone, email).
  - Sources: [Kol Zchut: cancelling an ongoing transaction](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%91%D7%99%D7%98%D7%95%D7%9C_%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%94_%D7%9E%D7%AA%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%AA); [Israel Consumer Council](https://www.consumers.org.il/category/deal-annulment-regulations)
  - An annual billing statement is sent in March. — [Consumer Protection Law, consolidated version (Wikisource)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%92%D7%A0%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A6%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%9F)
  - Distance-selling guide 2026. — [ziko](https://www.ziko.co.il/blog/hganat-hatzarchan-mischar-2026)
- **"OpenStudio".** NREL presents "OPENSTUDIO®" as a registered mark for energy-modeling software. The application is now maintained by the OpenStudio Coalition. — [NREL](https://www.nrel.gov/research/software/openstudio); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenStudio)
- **Trademark classes for apps.** Class 9 (downloadable software) and class 42 (SaaS). Search both. — [Gerben Law](https://www.gerbenlaw.com/?p=81)

### Inferences
- **The B2B model.** Galleries pay, so they will want a tax invoice, which means a licensed dealer. Invoices to galleries above NIS 5,000 will need an allocation number. Use invoicing software with an API (Green Invoice, iCount, etc.).
- **Subscription consumer protection.** It applies only to "consumers", that is private artists, not galleries. Still, build an online cancellation button, a renewal reminder, and an annual statement.
- **Trademark.** "Open Studio" is close to descriptive ("open studio" is a common term for open-studio events). Expect difficulty registering it, and a possible conflict in classes 9/42 with OpenStudio.
  - A registration may succeed only with a logo, a combined mark, or a more distinctive name.
  - Check the Israeli register (Israel Patent Office, trademarks database), TMview and WIPO Global Brand Database before investing in a brand.
  - Madrid allows designating the EU and the US from an Israeli application.

### Gaps
- No primary source in this round confirming VAT at 18% from 1.1.2025. It is widely known, but cite the Tax Authority in the report.
- I did not check prices and terms for Cardcom, Tranzila and Grow (Meshulam).
- No search was done of the Israeli trademark register or TMview for "Open Studio". A trademark attorney is required.
- I did not check whether a 2025–2026 amendment exists requiring cancellation "by any means" or a reminder before renewal. I found no source for it.
- The tax comparison between a VAT-exempt dealer and a company (corporate tax 23%, dividends) was not sourced. An accountant is required.

## 5. Funding: grants, programs, accelerators, crowdfunding

### Takeaway
- **Innovation Authority, Tnufa.** The most relevant source: a conditional grant of up to NIS 200,000 over 12 months (80% of the approved budget) for an early-stage entrepreneur or company. Repaid as royalties only on success.
- **Creative Europe.** Israel has participated in its Culture strand since 2022, through 2027, with a territorial exclusion. It is suited to cross-border cultural projects with European partners.
- **Lottery Council and Rabinovich Foundation.** In this round I found no current track in the Lottery Council or the Rabinovich Foundation that fits a tech platform. They mostly support artists and projects, not companies.

### Cited Findings
- **Tnufa.**
  - Up to NIS 200,000 over 12 months, 80% of the budget, for entrepreneurs and new companies. Funds proof of concept, prototype, IP and business development. — [Innovation Authority: Ideation (Tnufa)](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/programs/ideation-tnufa-incentive-program/); [Velo Labs 2026 guide](https://www.velo-labs.ai/resources/israel-innovation-authority-grants)
  - Older terms: NIS 100,000 a year for 2 years at 85%, now superseded. — [joltoo](https://joltoo.com/programs/il-iia-tnufa)
- **Thematic Tnufa challenges.** Grants vary (e.g. Critical Minerals 2026: NIS 1M for first place). — [Innovation Authority](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/calls_for_proposal/critical-minerals-challenge/)
- **Later tracks.**
  - Pre-Seed: 60% of a round up to NIS 2M.
  - Seed: 50% up to NIS 6M, raised in 7/2026.
  - Earlier caps of NIS 1.5M / 5M were also cited, so verify.
  - Sources: [Velo Labs](https://www.velo-labs.ai/resources/israel-innovation-authority-grants); [Science Business: stimulus package](https://sciencebusiness.net/network-updates/israeli-government-approves-transformative-stimulus-package-boost-high-tech); [Innovation Authority: Open for submission](https://innovationisrael.org.il/פתוחים-להגשה/)

  - Excludes the territories beyond the Green Line, East Jerusalem and the Golan.
  - Sources: [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/bythacqtc); [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.com/2022/06/13/settlements-excluded-from-eu-cultural-program-joined-by-israel); [Oireachtas (legal basis, Reg. 2021/818)](https://www.oireachtas.ie/en/debates/question/2022-06-21/75/); [JPost](https://jpost.com/israel-news/article-717638)
- **Lottery Council + Ministry of Culture.** Joint calls for artists in the Tkuma region (including visual art), about NIS 6.5M in total, published 17.3.2026. — [ICE](https://www.ice.co.il/consumerism/news/article/1106658)

### Inferences
- **Tnufa.** It requires an Israeli company or entrepreneur and real technological innovation. The automatic 3D pipeline (pose solve, geometry, light baking) is a strong angle. Strategy: open a company, submit Tnufa, and go on to Pre-Seed with an investor.
- **Culture funds.** The Lottery Council, the Ministry of Culture and the Rabinovich Foundation would mostly not fund the company. They could fund content projects: digital documentation of exhibitions together with a gallery or museum, as a partner or service provider.
- **Creative Europe.** Requires a consortium with European partners. Relevant to the later expansion stage.

### Gaps
