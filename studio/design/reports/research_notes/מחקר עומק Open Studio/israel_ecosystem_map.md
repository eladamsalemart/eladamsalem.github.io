# Israeli contemporary art ecosystem map, bottom-up (Tel Aviv first)

Research date: 2026-10-28. Method: Hebrew and English web search, plus direct downloads of primary pages: the Erev Rav exhibition guide, the Artists' Union PDF of state-supported galleries, the Fresh Paint catalog, Hebrew Wikipedia categories, gallery directories and municipal pages.

Access limits:
- WebFetch could not resolve erev-rav.com or timeout.co.il. Erev Rav was readable with curl through the proxy. timeout.co.il returned 403, so Time Out items below come from search-result summaries only.
- Instagram, Facebook and Haaretz full text were not readable, so **no follower counts could be verified**.

This file fills the gaps that `market_and_customers.md` and `gtm_cold_start.md` left open: gallery counts, artist counts and discovery channels. It does not repeat those files' figures (TAMA visits, the Fresh Paint 40K claim, Bezalel's 400–500 graduates, DataReportal platform reach).

## 1. Galleries and exhibition spaces: named list and counts (Tel Aviv-Yafo, Jerusalem, Haifa, rest of Israel)

### Takeaway
No official census of galleries exists. Two hard anchors do exist:
- **61 public (non-profit) galleries** received Ministry of Culture support in 2022, totalling **₪9.31M**. At least **16 of them are in Tel Aviv-Yafo** and about **11–13 in Jerusalem**.
- One snapshot of the Erev Rav exhibition guide (Oct 2026) lists about **26 Tel Aviv venues** with shows open at the same time.

Adding commercial galleries, art-school galleries, museums and project spaces from the directories gives a named Tel Aviv list of about **65–70 venues**. The realistic active universe is probably **80–120 exhibition spaces in Tel Aviv-Yafo** and roughly **250–400 nationally**. Both ranges are estimates (see Inferences). The densest cluster by far is **Kiryat HaMelacha** (Shvil HaMeretz and HaTavor streets in south Tel Aviv), where at least 12 venues sit within a few hundred metres.

### Inferences
- **Sizing the first atomic network:**
  - Tier A, high frequency: about 25–30 Tel Aviv venues running at once in Erev Rav's snapshot.
  - Tier B: about 16 state-supported Tel Aviv public and cooperative galleries.
  - Tier C: about 25–35 commercial galleries, from the directory names: Givon, Gordon, Rosenfeld, Zommer, Noga, Dvir, Braverman, Shalosh, Alon Segev, Hezi Cohen, Zemack, Engel, Stern, Rothschild Fine Art, Raw Art, Zimak, Binet, Litvak, Tedeschi and others.
  - Tier D: museums and art-school galleries.
  - That gives about **65–70 named Tel Aviv venues** from sources, or **80–120 active spaces** once unnamed project spaces are added. This is an estimate.
- **Weekly volume:** at about 6–10 shows per venue per year, 80–120 venues produce about **500–1,000 exhibitions a year in Tel Aviv**, or roughly **10–20 openings a week**. This estimate is consistent with Time Out's 17–27 picks a week, which are national but TLV-heavy.
- **One-walk launch cluster:** Shvil HaMeretz 2–6, HaTavor 32 and HaAmal 8/17 hold Artspace, Binyamin, Hanina, Maya, Dvir, Raw Art, Nolobaz, Cubia 6, the Shared Salon, the Artists' Studios, Artport and Benyamini. That is **12+ venues and about 300 artists in a few hundred metres**: the ideal "Gallery Weekend"-style first network.
- **The cooperative and non-profit tier is under financial stress** (Alfred closed; four others threatened). A free tool that documents shows (3D tour, archive) and brings an audience is especially valuable to them, but they have no money to pay. Commercial galleries are the paying tier.
- **National estimate:** 61 supported public galleries, plus about 60 museums (see Q5 and earlier notes), plus perhaps 100–200 commercial galleries, municipal galleries and project spaces, gives about 250–400 venues nationally. **This is an unverified estimate.**

### Gaps
- No official count of commercial galleries exists. The Israeli Art Galleries Association (איגוד הגלריות) did not surface in search, and it is unclear whether one exists today.
- The current (2025/2026) list of supported galleries was not found. The PDF is 2022 data.
- The Fresh Paint 2026 gallery exhibitor list was not published online in readable form.
- Haifa: almost no Haifa venues appeared in the support list or the directories. Haifa Museum, the Haifa University Art Gallery and the HaMoshava WIZO campus show up only through graduate shows. A Haifa count needs a separate search.
- Time Out and Haaretz full listings could not be fetched (403 and paywall).

## 2. Number of active visual artists in Israel

### Takeaway
No national count exists. The best proxies are:
- **1,200+ members** of the Professional Union of Visual Artists (איגוד האמנים הפלסטיים)
- **about 150 faculty** in the state-funded academic art departments
- **about 50 grants a year from the Plumes fund out of "many hundreds" of applicants**
- **120+ artists** who open studios at Tel Aviv's municipal "Loving Art. Making Art." event, and **about 300 artists** in Kiryat HaMelacha alone

A working estimate is **3,000–6,000 practising visual artists nationally**, of whom **about 1,000–2,000 are in the Tel Aviv area**. This is an estimate.

### Cited Findings
- Union membership: "over 1,200 artists have already joined us" (current projects page); about 1,150 in 2016, when the fee was ₪150 a year. Membership is open to professional artists who live in Israel and are over 20 — [Union projects page](https://igud-omanim.org/projects/); [search summary of igud-omanim.org](https://igud-omanim.org/%D7%90%D7%95%D7%93%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%95/)
- Union projects relevant to OPEN STUDIO:
  - 1% for Art law (2026)
  - mandatory artist fee in public galleries (2024)
  - artworks in public institutions (2025)
  - emergency studio subsidy (2024)
  - Be'eri Gallery in TLV (2023)
  - residency for evacuated artists in Jaffa (2023)
  - Source: [Union projects](https://igud-omanim.org/projects/)
- Union survey (2024) of **155 artists** who exhibited in public galleries:
  - about three quarters were not paid
  - a similar share paid out of pocket
  - **over 80% sold no work** as a result of the show
  - Source: [Erev Rav ch. 6](https://www.erev-rav.com/archives/60056)
- Plumes Fund (קרן פלומס): about 50 winners a year against "many hundreds" of applicants — [Erev Rav ch. 6](https://www.erev-rav.com/archives/60056)
- Curators: the Curators' Union surveyed about **250 curators** in 2020. 65% had worked unpaid, and the median annual income was under ₪30K — [Erev Rav ch. 6](https://www.erev-rav.com/archives/60056)
- Teaching is the main stable income. The art departments of state-funded academies (Bezalel, Shenkar, HaMidrasha, Kibbutzim College) have **about 150 faculty**, and roughly 10% teach in two places — [Erev Rav ch. 6](https://www.erev-rav.com/archives/60056)

  - An older edition had about 150 artists, 30 galleries and 4 museums — [search summary, tiuli](https://www.tiuli.com/events/13072/%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A4%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%97%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%90%D7%95%D7%94%D7%91%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%9C-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91)
  - The 2025 edition took place on 23–24 Oct 2025 (Eretz Israel Museum page) — [Eretz Israel Museum](https://www.eretzmuseum.org.il/%D7%90%D7%95%D7%94%D7%91%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA/)
  - The 2026 edition is planned for November — [Secret Tel Aviv, via search](https://secrettelaviv.com/magazine/blog/useful-info/best-events-in-tel-aviv-november-2026)
  - **Ramat HaSharon "Open Studio 2025"**: about 40 local artists — [Ramat HaSharon municipality](https://ramat-hasharon.muni.il/%D7%A1%D7%98%D7%95%D7%93%D7%99%D7%95-%D7%A4%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%97-2025-%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%9F/)
- Fresh Paint Greenhouse: about 50 emerging artists a year (51 in 2026) — [catalog](https://catalog.freshpaint.co.il/en/)

### Inferences
- The estimate's logic: 1,200 union members is a floor of self-identified professionals. Graduates add perhaps 300–600 fine-art and photography graduates a year nationally (Bezalel all departments 500+, Shenkar art about 33, plus HaMidrasha, Minshar, Musrara, Haifa, WIZO, Tel-Hai, Sapir, Kalisher, Avni and Oranim). Most leave practice within years. Together this gives **3,000–6,000 active artists**, about a third in Gush Dan. **Estimate, low confidence.**
- **Reachable artist "seed" pools:**
  - about 120 open-studio artists in the TLV municipal event
  - about 300 Kiryat HaMelacha artists
  - about 50 Fresh Paint Greenhouse artists a year
  - members of more than 10 cooperatives (each with about 10–16 members)
  - one graduating cohort a year from each school
  - Together these give about **500–800 named, reachable artists in Tel Aviv** for Phase 0/1 outreach.
- Artist economics are weak: over 80% sold nothing from a public gallery show. This supports a free artist tier, with value framed as exposure and documentation rather than sales.

### Gaps
- No CBS occupational count for "visual artists" was retrieved.
- Per-school graduate counts (HaMidrasha, Minshar, Musrara, Kalisher, WIZO Haifa, Tel-Hai, Sapir, Haifa University) were not found.
- The number of Ministry of Culture prize applicants was not found.
- Jaffa Port studios and Hayarkon studio counts were not found.

## 3. How Israeli audiences discover openings today

### Takeaway
Discovery is fragmented across about 6 kinds of channel:
1. **Erev Rav's "מדריך תערוכות"** plus its free weekly newsletter "רב-ערב"
2. weekly press round-ups (**Time Out**'s "X exhibitions for the weekend", **Haaretz**'s weekly "תערוכות מומלצות")
3. **gallery-owned** Instagram, Facebook, newsletters and WhatsApp (Alfred's note shows each cooperative runs its own)
4. English-language **Secret Tel Aviv**
5. event aggregators (**Makore / "מה קורה"**, 949 exhibitions listed nationally)
6. municipal sites and events

No dedicated Instagram account or public WhatsApp group that aggregates TLV openings was found. That is a gap in the market, though not proof that none exists.

### Cited Findings
- **Erev Rav** (independent art magazine, founded January 2010, edited by Ronen Eidelman and Yonatan Amir):
  - It keeps a continuously updated "מדריך תערוכות | עדכון תערוכות נפתחות ואירועים", split into "openings" by day and "running exhibitions" with closing dates. It covers about 35 shows nationally in one snapshot.
  - It also runs **"רב-ערב", a "weekly, free newsletter"** of selected art and culture events.
  - It has Twitter, Facebook and Instagram accounts.
  - Sources: [Erev Rav guide](https://www.erev-rav.com/israel-art-map); [Erev Rav ch. 6 footer](https://www.erev-rav.com/archives/60056)
- **Time Out Tel Aviv** publishes a weekly exhibitions round-up:
  - "אחרי הבחירות, בוחרים באמנות: 17 תערוכות לסופ"ש" (28.10.26)
  - "27 תערוכות לסופ"ש" (21.10.26)
  - "20 תערוכות מומלצות"
  - Listings give the opening day and hour (e.g. "Thursday 19:00")
  - Sources: [28.10](https://timeout.co.il/%D7%AA%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A4%D7%A9-281026); [21.10](https://timeout.co.il/%D7%AA%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%AA-211026); [20 shows](https://timeout.co.il/%D7%AA%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%AA-163/) (search summaries)

- **Gallery nights**: no Tel Aviv "ליל גלריות" or "gallery weekend" was found for 2025–2026. The city-wide art moments are:
  - "Loving Art. Making Art." (yearly, Sept–Nov)
  - White Night ("לילה לבן", 25 June 2026; art is one category among many)
  - Tel Aviv Festival (1–8 Nov 2026, with gallery and open-space wandering at TAMA)
  - Sources: [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/culture/stage/article/20817033); [Tel Aviv Festival](https://telavivfestival.co.il/) (search summaries)

### Inferences
- **Erev Rav's guide is the closest thing to a "source of truth" for openings, but it is a manually edited web page with no map, no follow and no reminders.** OPEN STUDIO's "map of current exhibitions" competes with and could partner with Erev Rav: data exchange, credit, or sponsoring the newsletter.
- Time Out's and Haaretz's 17–27 picks a week form a curated **upper bound on "notable" openings**. A comprehensive TLV feed would need about 10–20 openings a week, which matches the estimate in Q1.
- **No Tel Aviv gallery night exists** (unlike Berlin's GWB; see gtm_cold_start.md). OPEN STUDIO could create a monthly Kiryat HaMelacha "Thursday openings walk" with the 12+ venues there, at near-zero cost.
- Each cooperative maintains its own WhatsApp, newsletter and invitation design by volunteers. A tool that auto-generates the opening invite, link preview and post from one upload saves real labour, and that is a supply-side hook.

### Gaps
- No follower or subscriber counts were obtained for any Instagram, Facebook or newsletter (Erev Rav, Time Out, Portfolio, Secret TLV), because those platforms were blocked or not indexed.
- No public WhatsApp group or channel that aggregates openings was found. It may still exist, as an invite-only group.
- No survey exists of how Israeli gallery-goers learn about openings. The earlier recommendation of a founder survey at openings still stands.

## 4. Israeli art-tech and digital-exhibition attempts: what existed and what happened

### Takeaway
Israeli art-tech mostly targeted **global** markets: digital art display (Niio), recognition and discovery (Artbit), and logistics and marketplaces (ArtRunners, Artip, Artfully Walls). Several are dead (Artip closed in 2019; ArtRunners inactive since 2018). No Hebrew, local "follow artists plus exhibition map" product was found. COVID-era virtual efforts were museum-led and episodic. The newest precedent is **Fresh Paint 2026's QR-linked artist videos**, which is close to OPEN STUDIO's "studio clips".

### Cited Findings
- **Artbit**: founded in 2015 by Aric and Yoram Ben Zvi, raised about $700K. It offered image recognition of artworks by phone plus nearby venues and exhibitions, and let galleries and artists open profiles for its users — [NoCamels 2016 via search summary](https://nocamels.com/2016/07/israeli-startups-digitizing-art-world/). Current status unknown (the NoCamels URL returned 404 when fetched).
- **Niio** (Tel Aviv): a digital art streaming and display platform that raised a $15M Series A, partnered with Samsung, and has 6,000+ artists — [Calcalist Tech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3918127,00.html)
- **Artip**: ceased operations in Feb 2019. **ArtRunners**: presumed inactive since May 2018 — [Startup Nation Finder: Artip](https://finder.startupnationcentral.org/company_page/artip); [ArtRunners](https://finder.startupnationcentral.org/company_page/artrunners)
- **Artfully Walls**: an online art marketplace founded in 2012, based in Kfar Saba — [Startupill 2021](https://startupill.com/these-are-the-top-art-startups-in-israel-2021/) (search summary)
- **Hazut** (2026): an online registry and showcase for Israeli artists in Judea and Samaria, not a marketplace — [Cleveland Jewish News / JNS](https://www.clevelandjewishnews.com/jns/new-online-art-platform-showcases-artists-from-judea-and-samaria/article_6de25fee-0522-5de8-ad23-f17f092af5e5.html)
- **Israel Museum on Google Arts & Culture** since 2012, with 520 works online. In May 2020, during COVID closures, dozens of Israeli museums offered free virtual tours and online activities — [search summary of mako / hamichlol](https://www.hamichlol.org.il/Google_Art_Project); [mako](https://www.mako.co.il/news-entertainment/2020_q4/Article-8feacd568891571027.htm)
- **Artport's "Golden Howls"** (Gal Nissim): a free audio-walk app with a web-app fallback, used at the municipal art event, showing that institutions already use app-based art experiences — [Artport](https://www.artport.art/event/loving-art-making-art/)
- **Freshpaint 2026**: Portfolio and Teperberg produced personal "Meet the Artists" videos for all Greenhouse artists, with QR codes throughout the space — [Freshpaint catalog](https://catalog.freshpaint.co.il/en/)

### Inferences
- Earlier Israeli attempts failed or pivoted when they aimed at global B2B (logistics, recognition) without local network density. OPEN STUDIO's Tel Aviv-dense, Hebrew-first approach is different, and no local incumbent was found.
- Fresh Paint and Portfolio already pay to produce artist videos. They are both a **potential partner** (hosting those clips on artist pages) and proof that sponsors fund artist-content production.

### Gaps
- "Artsplash", "Arthena", "Artrendex" and an "Artist Dashboard" were not found as Israeli companies in this round. Arthena and Artrendex are generally known as US-based (unverified here).
- No usage data was found for Israeli museum virtual tours after COVID, nor whether TAMA has a Google Arts & Culture presence.

## 5. Museums and municipal bodies as partners (Tel Aviv first) and Digitel as a distribution channel

### Takeaway
The municipality is the strongest partner:
- it runs the annual "Loving Art. Making Art." (120+ open studios, 20 galleries, 4 museums)
- it runs the TA Tarbut centres in Kiryat HaMelacha and Da Vinci
- it funds Kav 16 and the Artists' Studios
- it runs the **Digitel** residents' club, which already pushes culture benefits through an app

Digitel's membership figure is old (">60K"; current numbers are much higher but not verified here). Bat Yam museums (MoBY), CCA, Artport, Artspace and Benyamini are state-supported non-profits that hold documentation budgets and audiences.

### Cited Findings
- **"אוהבים אמנות. עושים אמנות."**: the Tel Aviv-Yafo municipality's "big annual art event". In 2024 it ran 12–14 Sept with free entry; the main show was in Horshot Park, with projects at TA Tarbut centres, community centres and "אבץ" (the municipal public-art collection) — [Rashuiot](https://www.rashuiot.co.il/html5/ARCLookup.taf?_function=details&_ID=68738&did=1118&G=11234&SM=11234)
- **TA Tarbut Kiryat HaMelacha** (Shvil HaMeretz 6) is a municipal centre supported by the Tel Aviv Foundation; **TA Tarbut Da Vinci** hosts exhibitions — [@tatarbutkm](https://www.instagram.com/tatarbutkm/); [Erev Rav guide](https://www.erev-rav.com/israel-art-map)
- **Kav 16** is a municipal community gallery, founded 1998, ₪52K state support in 2022 — [Artbeat](https://www.artbeat.co.il/%D7%92%D7%9C%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%9C-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91); [Union PDF](https://igud-omanim.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Galleries.pdf)
- **Digitel (דיגיתל)**:
  - The municipal residents' club card and app offers a daily benefit, free entry to municipal culture institutions, discounts and a joining gift that can include free event tickets. Registration is free.
  - Older Time Out coverage cites "over 60 thousand residents" about a year after launch, so it is outdated.
  - Related programmes: "Digitel Nagish" (accessibility) and "Digitaf" (parents).
  - Sources: [Time Out Digitel](https://timeout.co.il/%d7%93%d7%99%d7%92%d7%99%d7%aa%d7%9c-%d7%90%d7%a4%d7%9c%d7%99%d7%a7%d7%a6%d7%99%d7%94/) (search summary); [Digitel terms](https://www.tel-aviv.gov.il/Residents/Digitel/Pages/Terms.aspx); [municipal mobile apps page](https://www.tel-aviv.gov.il/Transparency/Pages/MobileTLV.aspx)
- **Municipal venue search** exists at visit.tel-aviv.gov.il (already in gtm_cold_start.md, not repeated).
- Museums and institutions on the state gallery list:
  - Bat Yam Museums network (₪254K)
  - Digital Art Center Holon (₪265K)
  - CCA (₪253.6K)
  - Herzliya Artists' House (₪110.5K)
  - Rishon LeZion municipal gallery
  - Source: [Union PDF](https://igud-omanim.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Galleries.pdf)
- The **Ashdod Museum of Art** and the **Kiryat Tivon Israeli Art Gallery** were active in Oct 2026 — [Erev Rav guide](https://www.erev-rav.com/israel-art-map)
- **Tel Aviv Museum of Art**: "Alex Levac: Black Box" opened 27/10/2026 in the Eyal Ofer Pavilion. The museum also runs a newsletter — [TAMA](https://www.tamuseum.org.il/en/)
- **Eretz Israel Museum** hosted "Loving Art. Making Art." programming on 23–24 Oct 2025 (free, with registration) — [Eretz Israel Museum](https://www.eretzmuseum.org.il/%D7%90%D7%95%D7%94%D7%91%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA/)
- **Artists' Union**: its 2026 lobbying for a "1% for Art" law would create public-art commissions. The Union also set up Be'eri Gallery TLV — [Union projects](https://igud-omanim.org/projects/)

### Inferences
- **Partner priority for Tel Aviv:**
  1. the municipality culture department and the "Loving Art. Making Art." team: offer the official digital map and open-studio guide for the November event
  2. TA Tarbut Kiryat HaMelacha plus Artspace: a cluster pilot
  3. the Artists' Union (1,200 members; a newsletter channel to artists)
  4. Erev Rav (listings data and an audience of art insiders)
  5. Fresh Paint and Portfolio (artist videos, June peak)
  6. CCA, Artport and Benyamini (non-profits with residencies)
- **Digitel as a channel:** a free "art benefit" (e.g. a free 3D tour or priority entry to opening nights) listed in Digitel would reach residents through an official municipal app. This requires a municipal partnership; there is no self-serve path.
- Timing: the municipal event (Sept–Nov), Fresh Paint (June) and graduate shows (June–Aug) are the three annual peaks for onboarding both sides.

### Gaps
- No current Digitel member count was found (2025–2026).
- No contact or budget information for the Tel Aviv culture department's visual-arts unit was found.
- No data was found on whether Haifa, Jerusalem or Bat Yam have equivalent residents' apps.
- Israel Museum, Petach Tikva, Herzliya and Haifa museum partnership programmes were not researched in this round (the earlier notes cover TAMA).
