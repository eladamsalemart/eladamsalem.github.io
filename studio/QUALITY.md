# The owner's standard (read before any change; it grows with every lesson)

The owner should never have to ask twice for something a professional would have done unasked. Each request carries
the obvious things around it: infer them, do them, and check them before showing anything. When a lesson below was
learned the hard way, it is written as a rule, so no session learns it again.

## Before calling anything done: walk it as a visitor

1. Open it as a visitor does: phone size (390×844), no `?inspect`, from the way a visitor arrives (Home, the map,
   the museum door), not from a deep link.
2. Look at every new or moved thing from eye height, close, far, and from an angle (not only head-on). Ask: would a
   real gallery, a real museum, a real app of the best kind look like this? If not, it is not done.
3. Look at what is around the change too: what it touches, what it replaced, what it may have hidden or broken.
   After a patch, read its diff for lines it removed by accident (a slice once deleted the map's CSS: the "square" bug).
4. Tell the owner only what you saw working, and say plainly what was not checked (the real iPhone, the live site).
   A sub-session's report is not proof: look at the result before passing it on.

## Rules learned from the owner

**Real spaces, real logic.**
- A real exhibition joined to the museum keeps its own architecture. Its real openings (arches, doors, passages) are
  the ways in and out, linked to the museum. Never enter through a painting or a wall. No opening is left blocked,
  half-open or strange: each one either leads somewhere or is closed by a proper wall that looks built.
  (Ritual, 23.11: first entered through a painting, then through one arch while the second was blocked.)
- A room joins the museum only at the best level already reached anywhere in the app: textures, resolution, floor,
  light, sharpness. Measure it against the best room, not against "works". Below that level, it stays out.
- Things sit on what holds them: plants on the ground, works on walls, sculptures on plinths, signs on posts.

**Text on walls.**
- Wall text is on the wall: flush with its surface, inside the wall's bounds, never half in the air or past a corner
  or an opening. Check it from an angle. (Ritual's outside text, 23.11, hung half in the air.)
- "Clear" means really clear: the letters on the wall, no sheet, no thickness, no shadow. (The editor's wall text, 22.11.)

**Works of art.**
- Never crop a painting. White margins on the owner's photos are intentional.
- A work is shown at its real size and colour, lit as in a gallery.

**The app.**
- Phone first: every change is done only when it works on a phone.
- Home's look is fixed unless the owner asks in words.
- Design first, simple and uncluttered. When a look is in question, show options instead of guessing.
- The app stays in its own world (Studio Paper, the atlas map, the opening-night look).

**When a bug reaches the owner.**
- Fix it, find why it got through, and add the check (or the rule here) that would have caught it.
