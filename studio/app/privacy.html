Privacy

What Open Studio keeps about you, why, and how to have it deleted. Updated 2 November 2026.

Open Studio is an app for seeing art: artists' works at their real size, exhibitions you can walk through, and a map of what's on. It is made in Israel. You can reach us at ooopenstudio@gmail.com.

What we keep

Your email address
To sign you in. We send it a code or a link, and nothing else unless you ask.
Your name
Typed by you, or taken from your Google account if you sign in with Google.
Age, country, gender
Asked when you sign up with email, so what we show suits you. Gender can be “Prefer not to say”.
Artist or art lover
Which you chose, so the app opens the right way for you.

When you sign in with Google, Google tells us your name, your email address and your profile picture. We keep the name and the email. We never see your Google password, and we ask Google for nothing more.

What stays on your phone

The artists, shows and places you follow, and what you looked at last, are kept in your own browser, not on our servers. If you let the map use your location, it is used on your phone to show where you are and is never sent to us. A route you ask for sends its start and end points to the open routing service of OpenStreetMap.

Who sees it

  • Only you can read your details. Other people using Open Studio can't see them.
  • We don't sell your details, share them with advertisers, or show ads.
  • Your account is held by Supabase, the service that runs our sign-in and database. Sign-in emails are sent through Google's Gmail.
  • Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and map images from OpenFreeMap, which see your device's address the way any website does.

Deleting your account

Write to ooopenstudio@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we delete your account and everything kept with it within 30 days. You can change your details at any time under Account, “Change your details”. To clear what is kept on your phone, clear the site's data in your browser.

Children

Open Studio is for people aged 13 and over.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before it applies.
