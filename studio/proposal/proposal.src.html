The space · spec

Built to real scale

Each work hangs at the size recorded in its data, checked against its photo. Works whose stated size doesn't match the photo show “size unverified” on their label. In the app, that state blocks the work from going live until the artist fixes it.

1 unit = 1 mReal dimensions in cm, height × width × depth. Canvas depth 3.5 cm unless the artist states it.
145 cmCentre line. Works taller than 2 m sit 30 cm off the floor instead.
30° · 3000–3500 KTrack spots aimed 30° from vertical, warm white. Oils at gallery level, works on paper dimmer.
PBR Neutral · True colourKhronos tone mapping keeps colours true. One tap switches to even, flat light, so the photo's colour can be checked without the spot effect.
Eye at 160 cmEach work is viewed from about 1.5 × its longest side, never closer than 1.6 m.
≤ 15 MB first loadThe space loads first and works stream in 1k → 4k. A 2D walkthrough is always available.
Wall labelsTitle, year, medium, size and a red dot when sold. Placed at 145 cm, right of the work, as in a museum.
WebXRThe same space opens in VR on Quest and Vision Pro. Phones and laptops get guided 3D.
For the audience

Follow artists, not feeds

Five tabs: Home, Discover, City, Issues, Profile. The artist is always the unit. There are no likes, no infinite feed and no prices.

For artists · Studio for Artists

Apply, upload, hang

Entry is by application. Every work is checked by machine and then by a person before it goes live. The upload screen tells the artist exactly what to fix.

For the curatorial team

Review every work

Machine checks sort the queue, and a person decides. Every rejection says what to fix.
