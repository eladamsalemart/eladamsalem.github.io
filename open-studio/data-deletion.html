Deleting your data

How to have Open Studio delete your account, and anything it brought in from Instagram. Updated 3 November 2026.

Delete everything

Write to ooopenstudio@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my data”. Within 30 days we delete your account, your details, your studio and its works, and anything brought in from Instagram, and we write back to say it's done.

If you connected Instagram

When an artist connects their Instagram account, Open Studio keeps the posts they choose to bring into their studio (the pictures and their captions) and a key from Instagram that lets it read them. To take that away:

  1. Delete the works you no longer want from your studio in the app (Studio → Works → a work → Delete).
  2. Remove Open Studio from Instagram: in the Instagram app, Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → Open Studio → Remove. Open Studio can then no longer read your account, and the key it kept stops working.
  3. To have the rest deleted too, write to us as above.

What stays on your phone

The artists, shows and places you follow, and what you looked at last, are kept in your own browser. To clear them, clear this site's data in your browser's settings.
